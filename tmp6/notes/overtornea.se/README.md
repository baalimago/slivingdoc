# overtornea.se chain notes

- This chain may be handed Kommunfullmäktige protocols of municipalities other
  than Övertorneå (e.g. Öckerö kommun, whose protocols header with
  "ÖCKERÖ KOMMUN ... Kommunfullmäktige" and NetPublicator layout). The protocol
  text is authoritative: derive organization_name/date/decisions from the
  document even when a persisted meeting for a different date/municipality
  (contamination) is shown. Re-derive the whole meeting on contamination
  sweeps.
- Do not reject such documents: they are Kommunfullmäktige meetings of the
  expected type; only meeting-type mismatch warrants rejected: true.
