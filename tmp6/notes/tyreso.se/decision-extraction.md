# Tyresö KF decision extraction guidance

- Minoritetsåterremiss (minority remand): a counted votering may show a
  majority voting "ja" to decide the matter today (e.g. 38 ja / 20 nej /
  2 avstår), yet the recorded beslut is still "återremitteras via
  minoritetsåterremiss" — a ≥1/3 minority can force a remand under Swedish
  municipal law. Record the FINAL decision (the återremiss) as outcome, and
  put the vote counts and the question phrasing in voting_method. Do not
  mistake the vote result for the outcome.
- Valärenden (avsägelser och fyllnadsval) is one § with many numbered
  beslut points; keep as one decision entry with full text of all points.
  No counted vote → omit voting_method.
