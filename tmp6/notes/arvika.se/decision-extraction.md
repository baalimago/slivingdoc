# arvika.se scanner notes (Arvika kommun)

## Cross-municipality protocols in this chain
- The arvika.se chain can carry Kommunfullmäktige protocols of other municipalities.
  A contamination sweep flagged one whose organization ("Aneby kommun") does not name
  the chain target Arvika; internal place references in the protocol confirmed Aneby
  kommun, so the flag was a false positive. Convention (same as the boden.se/Berg and
  Nordanstig entries in canonicalization-worklog.md): the protocol text is authoritative
  for organization_name — do not rename the organization to the chain target, and a
  municipality mismatch alone is not grounds for rejection (the meeting type is still
  Kommunfullmäktige). When in doubt, consult the other municipality's notes directory
  (here: aneby.se) for its extraction conventions.
