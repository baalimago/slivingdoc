# Östra Göinge KF decision extraction guidance

- Signed-format KF protocols (Instans: Kommunfullmäktige) list decisions per § under a
  "Kommunfullmäktiges beslut" heading; keep all bullets of a § in one decision entry,
  paragraph_number as "§NN".
- Include procedural opening items (§ Val av justerare, § Godkännande av dagordning) —
  they record explicit decision outcomes.
- Skip Meddelanden-to-KF items (beslut: "Redovisningen godkänns") — announcement items,
  not substantive decisions. Same for free-standing "Information" sections (no § number).
- Interpellation items are handled as formal decisions (e.g. svaret noteras och läggs till
  handlingarna) — include them.
- Jäv noted per § (e.g. "§ 48 jäv" next to a name) is not a decision; mention in the
  decision summary if relevant, not as a separate entry.
- Politicians: the "Beslutande" roster on page 1 with party abbreviations (M, S, C, V, KD,
  SD); roles only where printed (Ordförande, 1:e vice ordförande, 2:e vice ordförande).
  Ej tjänstgörande ersättare are not decision-makers; skip them.
- No voting_method unless an omröstning with result is printed.
