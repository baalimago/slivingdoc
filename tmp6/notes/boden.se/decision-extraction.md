# boden.se chain — decision extraction guidance

- Chain target is Boden, but this chain has repeatedly received Kommunfullmäktige
  protocols from OTHER municipalities (observed: Bergs kommun, Avesta kommun).
  The document text is authoritative: derive organization_name / organization_level /
  organization_location from the protocol itself, never from the chain target, and do
  NOT reject solely because the municipality differs from Boden. Reject only when the
  meeting type does not match the expected type.
- Attendance/vote list layout used in these protocols (närvaro- och omröstningslista):
  "X" in the Närvaro column marks presence; a number in the "Ers" column next to a
  ledamot row identifies the ersättare (by their number in the ersättare list) who
  substituted. Empty JA/NEJ/AVS columns mean no recorded roll-call vote — do not invent
  votes. Include present ersättare in politicians with role "ersättare"; omit party
  when the list gives none.
- Bodens kommun's own KF protocols (2024 layout) use party abbreviations "BA" and
  "SJVP" in attendance lists and yrkanden without ever spelling the party names out.
  No canonical expansion is observable in the source; submit these verbatim rather
  than guessing an expansion.
